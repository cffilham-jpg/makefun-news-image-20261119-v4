## The infographic

A single 1024×1024 image that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in seven
stories. The biggest story gets a wide hero panel at the top, a dotted
world map sits underneath with numbered pins marking where each story
happened, and below that six colour-coded cards in a 3×2 grid carry one
headline, a short report and one key stat each. Every number on the image
was checked against a primary source for the day itself — nothing is
carried over from an earlier day's reporting.

## The seven stories

1. **Trilateral first.** Trump, Xi and Putin sit down together for the first
   time — 90 minutes of talks at APEC in Shenzhen — and leave with a
   four-point "peace declaration" on nuclear risks, energy and UN reform.
   Ukraine is not mentioned in it.
   *Source: USA Today, 19 Nov 2026.*
2. **ICJ verdict.** The International Court of Justice rules 9–6 in The
   Gambia v. Myanmar — the first state ever found guilty of genocide — over
   the 2017 campaign against the Rohingya.
   *Source: UN News, 19 Nov 2026.*
3. **Iran war vote.** The US Senate rejects the war-powers resolution that
   would have ended the Iran war, 51–48 — the first Iran vote since the
   midterms.
   *Source: Reuters, 19 Nov 2026.*
4. **Suneung day.** 551,864 students sit South Korea's nine-hour college
   entrance exam; a nationwide emergency alert exposes 22 concealed
   smartphones. No AI cheating devices are found.
   *Source: Arirang, 19 Nov 2026.*
5. **Cyclone Akvan.** The storm closes on India's east coast under an IMD red
   alert, with landfall expected between Narsapur and Puri by noon Friday
   and gusts reaching 110 km/h.
   *Source: India Meteorological Department, National Bulletin No. 29,
   0900 IST 19 Nov 2026.*
6. **Anthropic IPO.** ANT opens at $111.50 and closes at $97.17, up 17% on
   the day, after an $81.3bn raise — the largest IPO on record.
   *Source: Nasdaq quote, trading day 19 Nov 2026.*
7. **GTA VI is out.** The game launches worldwide at midnight in Leonida;
   early reviews sit at 99/100 and 7.9 million people watch live at peak
   across all platforms — the biggest game launch stream ever.
   *Sources: Le Monde, 19 Nov 2026; Streams Charts day-one recap,
   19 Nov 2026.*

## What changed vs v3

- **Seven stories instead of six.** The US Senate's 51–48 Iran war-powers
  vote is new — the Middle East was entirely absent from v3 — and the grid
  is now a balanced 3×2 instead of an asymmetric 3+2.
- **Two facts corrected.** The Suneung card no longer claims 98 students
  were caught with phones: the verified figure is 22 smartphones exposed
  by the emergency alert, with no AI devices found. The GTA VI card now
  reports the real day-one peak of 7.9 million concurrent viewers across
  all platforms, not the 2.3 million Twitch-only figure from early in the
  day.
- **Sharper wording.** The Akvan card says gusts to 110 km/h (the gust
  figure, not sustained wind), the ICJ card names the ruling as the first
  of its kind, and the hero subtext now states plainly that Ukraine is not
  mentioned in the declaration.
